Choose your program file

In Arduino IDE click Sketch → Export Compiled Binary. Then choose the file that ends in .ino.bin.

No file chosen yet.
Where is the file?

Inside your sketch folder, in build/esp32.esp32.esp32s3/. For example, for a sketch called MySquare it is MySquare/build/esp32.esp32.esp32s3/MySquare.ino.bin. Don't choose .merged.bin, .bootloader.bin or .partitions.bin.

Tip: in Arduino IDE use Sketch → Show Sketch Folder to open the folder.

3

Upload

After you change your code: Export in Arduino IDE again, then click Upload here.
